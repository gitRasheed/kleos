# Studio meter fixtures

The Mozart and Beethoven WAVs are synthesized opening excerpts of Symphony No. 40, K. 550 and Symphony No. 5, Op. 67. The Beethoven transcription used the [Mutopia orchestral parts](https://github.com/MutopiaProject/MutopiaProject/tree/master/ftp/BeethovenLv/O67/Symphony5_1/Symphony5_1-lys). The Mozart transcription adapted concert pitches and durations from Gaylon Babcock’s [full score, IMSLP #955212](https://s9.imslp.org/files/imglnks/usimg/6/6b/IMSLP955212-PMLP01572-Symphony_Number_40_K550_Full_Score_concert_pitch.pdf), licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The flat-top fixture clips the Mozart waveform at ±0.074, then applies gain to reach −16 integrated LUFS. The sine, saw and square WAVs are synthesized clean controls.
